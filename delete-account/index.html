Request Account Deletion

If you have a GAP Connect account and would like it permanently deleted, you can submit a deletion request by email. Account deletion is carried out by the GAP Connect administration team; users cannot delete their accounts directly.

How to request deletion

  1. Email hello@gapconnect.au from the email address associated with your GAP Connect account, if possible.
  2. Use the subject line GAP Connect – Account Deletion Request.
  3. State that you want your GAP Connect account and associated personal data permanently deleted.
  4. Our administration team will review the request, verify account ownership if necessary, and process the deletion.
Request Account Deletion

What happens to your data?

When your deletion request is approved and processed, your account will be permanently removed along with associated personal information that is no longer required. Certain records, such as donation or financial transaction records, may need to be retained where required by applicable law or legitimate record-keeping obligations. Any retained information will be handled according to applicable privacy requirements.

Important: Please do not include your password, identity documents, or other sensitive information in your initial email. Our team will contact you if additional verification is needed.

Need help?

For questions about your account deletion request, contact hello@gapconnect.au.
