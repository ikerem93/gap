Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Last updated: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GAZA AUSTRALIAN PROGRAM LTD (ABN 51 680 212 453) (“GAP”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, discloses and protects personal information through the GAP Connect mobile app for iOS and Android, its web version, and the systems that support them (together, the “Service”).

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Where other privacy laws apply to you, such as the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we also respect the rights those laws give you.

In short: we collect only what we need to run our assistance programs, donations, volunteering, partnerships and events. GAP Connect has no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

GAP Connect is operated by GAZA AUSTRALIAN PROGRAM LTD (ABN 51 680 212 453), an Australian organisation that supports Palestinian families in Australia through assistance programs, donations, volunteering, partnerships and community events.

We are responsible for the personal information collected through the Service (under the GDPR we are the “controller”). Our registered address is [ORGANISATION ADDRESS]. Our contact details are in section 18.

2. Information we collect

What we collect depends on how you use GAP Connect. You can browse public content and donate as a guest without an account.

2.1 Account and profile details

  • First and last name, email address, phone number, residential address and state.
  • Date of birth (for beneficiary accounts).
  • Account type (for example beneficiary, donor, volunteer or partner) and the roles you hold in the app.
  • Organisation name (for donors and partners).
  • Your password, stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash. We cannot see your password.
  • An optional profile photo.

2.2 Household and family details

  • Household size, and the names and relationships of household members you choose to list.
  • For support requests, the names, relationships and ages of family members included in the request.

2.3 Identity and residency verification (beneficiaries)

  • Your residency or visa status (for example citizen, permanent resident, temporary, bridging or humanitarian visa).
  • Copies of identity documents (such as a passport) and visa documents you upload, and the outcome of our verification review.

2.4 Support requests

  • Rental assistance: rent amount and frequency, whether rent is overdue, landlord name and contact details, your reasons for applying, a rental or lease agreement, bank statements and, where relevant, your partner’s bank statements and other supporting files.
  • Business assistance: business name, sector, years operating, what support you need and supporting documents.
  • Employment assistance: skills, availability, city and supporting information.
  • Messages and attachments exchanged with our team about a request.
  • Records our authorised staff create while handling a request, such as assessment notes, recommended and approved amounts, decisions and assistance payment records.

2.5 Donations

  • Donor name and email (optional for guest donations), amount, currency, payment method, status, reference and any note you add.
  • Bank transfer receipts you upload as proof of payment.
  • For card donations, the Stripe Checkout session reference and payment status. Card details are entered on Stripe’s secure payment page. We never receive or store your full card number or security code.

2.6 Volunteering, partnerships and events

  • Volunteer applications (skills and availability) and partnership requests (organisation and message).
  • Event registrations: name, email, phone, number of attendees, any notes you add, and our review of the registration.

2.7 Device and technical information

  • Push notification token, device platform (iOS, Android or web) and app language, used to send you notifications.
  • Installation identifier: a random ID created by the app on your device and stored in its secure storage. It links guest donations to that installation so you can see your donation history without an account.
  • Your notification preferences and which in-app updates you have read.
  • Our servers and hosting providers keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, request time and error details) to keep the Service secure and working.

2.8 Information stored only on your device

Your sign-in token is kept in your device’s secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). If you add an event to your calendar, the app saves a reference to that calendar entry on your device so it can tell you the event is already saved. Your calendar data is not sent to us.

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, browsing history, or audio recordings, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

3. How we collect information

  • From you, when you create an account, complete your profile, apply for a role, submit a request, upload documents, send us messages, donate, apply to volunteer or partner, or register for an event.
  • From our staff, who record assessment notes, decisions and payments while handling your request.
  • From Stripe, which tells us whether a card payment succeeded.
  • Automatically from your device, such as your push notification token and installation identifier.

If you give us information about another person (for example a family member, partner or landlord), please make sure they know you are sharing it with us and why. When you submit a request, you confirm that you have permission to provide the information and documents it includes.

5. Sensitive information and consent

Identity and visa documents, residency status, family circumstances and financial records are highly personal. Some of this may be, or may reveal, sensitive information under the Privacy Act (for example information about nationality, racial or ethnic origin), or special category data under the GDPR.

We only collect sensitive information with your consent and where it is reasonably necessary for our programs. You give consent when you choose to upload these documents and confirm the declaration in the app. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us, but we may then be unable to assess or continue your request.

Identity, visa and financial documents can only be seen by authorised staff whose role requires it (for example assessors, finance officers and approvers).

7. Push notifications and email

If you allow notifications, the app registers a push token with our server together with your platform and app language. We use it to send notices about your account, requests, payments, events and important alerts. Notifications are delivered through the Expo push service, which passes them to Apple Push Notification service (iOS) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android).

You can choose which categories you receive (alerts, requests, payments, events and account) in the app’s notification settings, or turn notifications off in your device settings. When you sign out, the app removes its push token from our server. Notices are also saved in your in-app updates.

We send service emails, such as password reset codes, donation recovery codes and staff invitations, through our email provider. These are not marketing emails.

9. International transfers

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Australia. Our database and file storage are hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Render, Stripe, Google, Expo and Apple may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

Before disclosing information overseas, we take reasonable steps under APP 8 to make sure it is protected, including by using reputable providers with appropriate security and privacy commitments. Where the GDPR applies, transfers rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses offered by those providers.

10. How we protect information

  • All data between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS).
  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. One-time codes are stored only as hashes and expire.
  • Uploaded files are kept in a private storage bucket. They are never public and can only be reached through our signed-in API, which checks your permissions on every request.
  • Staff access is role-based, so each person only sees what their role needs.
  • Your sign-in token is kept in your device’s secure storage, and we limit repeated sign-in and code attempts.

No system is completely secure. If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, and any other regulator where the law requires it.

11. How long we keep information

  • Account and profile information: while your account is active, and deleted or de-identified after you ask us to delete it, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Support requests, assistance payments and donation records: for as long as Australian tax, accounting and charity record-keeping laws require, even after an account is closed.
  • Uploaded documents: while needed for your request or verification, then deleted or kept only as long as the law requires.
  • Push tokens: until you sign out, turn off notifications or the token stops working.
  • Password reset and recovery codes: short-lived and expire after use or a short time.

When we no longer need information, we take reasonable steps to securely destroy or de-identify it.

12. Your rights and choices

  • Access: ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: update your profile in the app at any time, or ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, out of date or incomplete.
  • Documents: remove documents you uploaded from the Documents centre. Identity and visa documents that are under review or approved cannot be removed in the app; contact us for help.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your account and personal information (see section 13).
  • Withdraw consent: at any time, without affecting processing that already happened.
  • Notifications: choose categories in the app or turn notifications off in your device settings.
  • Anonymity: browse and donate as a guest. Support requests require us to identify you.
  • Additional GDPR rights (if they apply to you): restrict or object to processing, data portability, and complain to your local data protection authority.

We respond within a reasonable time, usually within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first. Making a request is free.

13. Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your GAP Connect account and the personal information linked to it at any time:

  1. Email admin@gazaaustralian.org from the email address on your account, with the subject “GAP Connect account deletion request”.
  2. Tell us your full name and say you want your account deleted.
  3. We will confirm the request (and may ask you to verify your identity), then delete your account within 30 days and email you when it is done.

What we delete: your account and profile, profile photo, household details, uploaded identity, visa and other documents, push tokens, notification preferences and in-app updates.

What we may keep: records we must keep by law, such as donation records and records of assistance requests and payments, for the period the law requires. We keep these securely, restrict access to them, and delete or de-identify them when the period ends.

Guest donors can ask us to delete donation details linked to their email address the same way. Uninstalling the app removes data stored on your device, but does not delete your account on our servers.

14. Children

GAP Connect accounts are intended for people aged 18 and over. The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly let children create accounts. We collect information about children only when a parent or guardian provides it, for example in household details or a support request. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
